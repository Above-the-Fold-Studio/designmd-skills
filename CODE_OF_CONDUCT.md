# Code of conduct

DesignMD Skills is a professional collaboration space. Contributors,
maintainers, and participants must:

- communicate respectfully and critique work rather than people;
- welcome good-faith questions and different levels of experience;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
  doxxing, and sustained disruption;
- respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and security-reporting boundaries;
- disclose relevant conflicts when reviewing licensing or commercial access.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that
violate these expectations. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented,
and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[hello@designmd.co](mailto:hello@designmd.co). Include links or screenshots when
safe, but do not expose another person's private information. Maintainers will
acknowledge reports, limit access to people needed for review, and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere.
