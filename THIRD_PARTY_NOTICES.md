# Third-party notices

No third-party source is bundled in the governance bootstrap.

Future adapted entries must add a notice in the same pull request that adds the
source. The notice must identify the upstream author, repository and exact path,
pinned commit or release, license, required attribution, DesignMD modifications,
and any excluded assets or dependencies.

A curated link does not copy upstream source, but its provenance record must
still identify the destination and the date its license and availability were
verified.

Entries classified as `license-review-required` or
`redistribution-prohibited` cannot be included in a public generated package.
