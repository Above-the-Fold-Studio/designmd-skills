# Design System Audit

## Outcome

An evidence-based view of where a design system is coherent, where product
usage diverges, and which changes will improve the most surfaces safely.

## Use it when

- Tokens, components, and documentation disagree.
- Product teams repeatedly bypass or fork system components.
- A migration or consolidation needs a risk-based sequence.
- Accessibility problems may originate in shared defaults.

## Design approach

Audit both supply and consumption. A beautifully documented component that no
product uses is not a successful system, while a product exception may expose a
missing capability rather than noncompliance.

Keep diagnosis separate from remediation. Large systems need ownership,
deprecation, migration, and verification plans before broad mechanical changes.

## Example request

> Audit our tokens, button and form components, documentation, and three product
> consumers. Separate system defects from adoption debt and exceptions.

## Optional DesignMD enhancement

DesignMD can provide authorized private catalog and conformance evidence. The
standalone audit works from repositories and artifacts available to the agent.
